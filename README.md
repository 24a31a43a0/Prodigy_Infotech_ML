## 📌 Task Details

### 🏠 PRODIGY_ML_01 — House Price Prediction
- Implemented **Linear Regression** to predict house sale prices
- Features used: Square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, garage area, year built, overall quality
- Evaluation: RMSE and R² Score
- Dataset: Kaggle House Prices Advanced Regression Techniques

---

### 🛒 PRODIGY_ML_02 — Customer Segmentation
- Built **K-Means Clustering** (K=5) to segment retail customers
- Based on Annual Income and Spending Score
- Used Elbow Method and Silhouette Score to find optimal K
- Dataset: Mall Customer Segmentation Data

---

### 🐱🐶 PRODIGY_ML_03 — Cats vs Dogs Classification
- Implemented **Support Vector Machine (SVM)** with RBF kernel
- Applied PCA for dimensionality reduction before SVM training
- Achieved strong classification accuracy on image data
- Dataset: Kaggle Dogs vs Cats

---

### 🖐️ PRODIGY_ML_04 — Hand Gesture Recognition
- Developed a **CNN model** to classify 10 different hand gestures
- Used BatchNormalization, Dropout for regularization
- Applied EarlyStopping and ReduceLROnPlateau callbacks
- Dataset: LeapGestRecog (Kaggle)

---

### 🍕 PRODIGY_ML_05 — Food Recognition & Calorie Estimation
- Built a **Transfer Learning model** using MobileNetV2 (ImageNet weights)
- Recognizes 10 food categories and estimates calorie content per 100g
- Used ImageDataGenerator for data augmentation
- Dataset: Food-101 (Kaggle)
